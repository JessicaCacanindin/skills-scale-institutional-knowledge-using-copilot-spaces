# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This documentation describes OctoAcme’s lightweight, iterative approach to delivering cross-functional projects. Work starts with initiation: confirm the business need and measurable outcomes, align stakeholders, and decide whether to proceed. Planning turns approved work into prioritized scope, milestones, dependencies, resourcing, and a Definition of Done.

During execution, teams use a shared project board, regular standups and delivery syncs, small reviewed pull requests, and demos to track progress and surface blockers. Risks and dependencies are recorded, reviewed regularly, and escalated when needed; weekly team coordination and stakeholder updates keep decisions and status visible. Quality checks include automated tests, CI and security scans, and manual QA where appropriate.

Before release, teams confirm acceptance criteria, passing checks, release notes, and rollback plans, then deploy, verify, and communicate the result. Retrospectives capture lessons and owned action items that feed into future work. Roles are explicit: Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; Developers build and test; QA validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals.
